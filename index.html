Treatments

One goal, several ways to get there.

Every eye is different. These are the vision-correction options our specialists can match to yours.

Nearsightedness, farsightedness, astigmatism

Femto-LASIK

A femtosecond laser creates a thin corneal flap, then an excimer laser reshapes the cornea to correct your prescription. Most people notice clearer vision within a day.

Ask about Femto-LASIK →
Nearsightedness and astigmatism

ReLEx SMILE

A flapless, keyhole approach: the laser shapes a tiny lens of tissue inside the cornea, removed through a small incision. A good fit for active lifestyles.

Ask about ReLEx SMILE →
High prescriptions or thin corneas

ICL lens implants

A thin collamer lens is placed inside the eye, in front of your natural lens. No corneal tissue is removed, and the lens can be taken out if needed.

Ask about ICL lens implants →
Clouded natural lens

Cataract surgery

The cloudy lens is replaced with a clear intraocular lens. Premium lens options can also reduce your need for glasses at different distances.

Ask about Cataract surgery →
Thinning, cone-shaped cornea

Keratoconus care

Corneal cross-linking strengthens the cornea to slow progression, with ring segments or specialty lenses when the stage calls for them.

Ask about Keratoconus care →
